Sesterzo is a simple budgeting app meant to be simple to use, secure, and totally free and open source.

It allows you to create (and soon, be part of) up to 5 budgets. For each budget, you can set your
recurring incomes, savings, and expenses that happen each month but also the ones that are specific
for a single month.

Entries (actual expenses, savings, or additional incomes) are automatically grouped together based on
the label you assign, and the app will show you a summary of your finances based on that.

All data concerning your expenses and your budgets are end-to-end encrypted, so only you have access to them.
No data whatsoever about the user are collected.
